Accolade scope: Displayed accolade rows cover major-league championships where applicable, MVP, All-Star, All-League, All-Defensive, DPOY, 75th Anniversary Teams, and Hall of Fame status. alternate-league awards and missing categories such as Finals MVP, Rookie of the Year, scoring titles, rebounding titles, and assist titles are not displayed in this template and should not be implied.
